# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Crypto traders and curious newcomers on Stellar testnet: hackathon judges, Stellar community members, and memecoin degens used to pump.fun. They browse a list of coins, open one, and buy or sell within seconds, usually on a laptop or a phone.

## Product Purpose
A memecoin launchpad where every coin's bonding-curve reserve is a tokenized real-world asset (CETES, US treasuries, NVDA) instead of XLM. The app has to feel as fast and familiar as pump.fun: discover coins, see live activity, trade, launch your own. It also has to show what makes Garfio different: the RWA backing and its yield. Success means a 3-minute demo with no friction: log in, faucet, create, buy, and watch the meme rise with the RWA.

## Brand Personality
No branding yet. Neutral, information-first layout. Familiar memecoin-launchpad patterns (live trades feed, king of the hill, token cards, bonding-curve progress, trade panel) placed well. Visual identity comes later.

## Anti-references
- A finance terminal or a SaaS dashboard: too serious, no memecoin energy in the structure.
- Generic crypto landing pages: hero copy and stats with no tokens to look at.
- Anything that hides the trade action or makes users read before they can act.

## Design Principles
1. Tokens first: the first screen is coins and live activity, not explanations.
2. Earned familiarity: copy proven launchpad patterns (pump.fun) so traders know where everything is.
3. Show the backing: every coin surfaces its RWA pair, reserve and yield next to price and market cap.
4. Act in one place: the trade panel is always visible on the coin page.
5. Identity is cheap: every wallet and coin gets a recognizable avatar and name with zero setup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-reachable controls, visible focus, reduced-motion fallbacks for the live feed. Buy and sell are not distinguished by color alone (labels and signs too).
